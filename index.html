About the project

Keeping songs accessible, with care for where they come from.

Rongmei Hymnal helps preserve and share the songs that carry faith, language, and community memory. Each songbook remains a distinct, carefully credited collection, with its editorial, publication, source, and rights information kept alongside the songs.
