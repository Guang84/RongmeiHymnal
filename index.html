About the project

A digital hymn library developed by GLab Enterprise.

Rongmei Hymnal brings together digital editions of Rongmei gospel songbooks and selected English hymn collections. Each collection retains its own identity, source details, contributor credits, editorial notes and rights information. Developed by GLab Enterprise, the project aims to make these collections easier to explore and read while respecting the people and communities responsible for the original works.

01Easy to explore

Find a song by its title or number, then open its individual source record.

02Faithful to each book

Every collection retains its own identity, credits, sources, and editorial notes.

03Made to endure

The library uses a versioned per-song data structure so collections can grow without downloading every lyric when the homepage opens.
